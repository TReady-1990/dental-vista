Meet Your Dentist

Dr. Inesha BakerDMD · OWNER

Dr. Baker has spent more than ten years helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles. Patients know her for a gentle, compassionate approach. She takes time to hear each person's goals and concerns, so every visit feels comfortable and stress-free.

She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Medical University of South Carolina and completed advanced training in implants and digital dentistry. She keeps up with continuing education to bring modern, patient-centered care and current technology to every chair.

Outside the office, Dr. Baker loves to travel, explore Charleston's food scene, and spend time with her family here in the Lowcountry.
